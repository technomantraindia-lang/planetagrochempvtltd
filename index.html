ABOUT US

PLANET AGRO CHEM PVT LTD.

Planet Agro Chem is a trusted Indian exporter and supplier of high-quality agricultural commodities. Rooted in India’s rich agrarian landscape, we connect local farmers and certified producers directly with international buyers across the globe. We handle end-to-end sourcing, stringent quality grading, packaging, and smooth export logistics. Our goal is simple: to deliver farm-fresh, pure, and export-standard Indian agro products on time, every time.

Direct Farm Connectivity

Directly bridging Indian farming communities and certified producers with global markets.

End-to-End Execution

Complete supervision from farm sourcing and rigorous grading to export packaging and container dispatch.
