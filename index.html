PRECISION FIVE-STAGE HANDLING FOR ACCURATE AGRO EXPORT DELIVERY

Direct farm procurement, Buhler color sorting, multi-tier lab assay, and climate-controlled staging engineered for zero port rejections across 35+ destination countries.

Explore Full Quality Protocol >

Our closed-loop handling process ensures safe, certified, and on-time agro delivery to 35+ destination ports.

01/ SOURCING & RESIDUE ↗

Direct farm-gate procurement from certified cultivator cooperatives across Gujarat with pre-harvest residue checks.

02/ SORTEX & QUALITY GRADING ↗

Multi-stage magnetic destoning, gravity separation, and lab titration for moisture, curcumin, aflatoxin, and pesticide traces.

03/ EXPORT PACKAGING ↗

Multi-wall kraft paper, airtight PP bags, and desiccated container stuffing at Mundra and Kandla ports with live GPS tracking.

04/ SILO & COLD STORAGE ↗

Climate-controlled humidity-regulated silos preserving volatile oil content and preventing fungal development during staging.

05/ OCEAN PORT DISPATCH ↗

Direct port-gate clearance at Mundra & Kandla ports with complete phytosanitary, fumigation, and SGS inspection certificates.
