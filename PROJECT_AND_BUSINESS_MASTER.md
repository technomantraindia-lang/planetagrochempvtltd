## 1. Executive Summary

**Planet Agro Chem Pvt. Ltd.** is an Indian premier merchant exporter and agricultural supply-chain powerhouse headquartered in Gujarat, India. With more than two decades of dedicated trade excellence, the company bridges the gap between India’s agrarian harvest basins and international industrial food conglomerates, wholesale repackers, spice extractors, and retail brand owners across **35+ countries**.

The company operates an end-to-end integrated supply model encompassing:
* **12,000+ Contracted Acres** of ethical cultivation partnerships across Gujarat, Rajasthan, and northern grain belts.
* **Automated Buhler Multi-Camera Optical Sortex Lines** providing optical defect separation with <0.01% impurities and 99.5%–99.9% purity standards.
* **Dual NABL-Accredited Quality Testing Labs** guaranteeing strict compliance with European Union (EU), United States FDA, GCC, and Codex Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs).
* **72-Hour Rapid Port Staging** at India's foremost deep-water container ports: Mundra, Kandla, and Nhava Sheva (JNPT).
* **50,000+ Metric Tons (MT)** in annual export volume across grains, whole spices, pulses, oilseeds, dehydrated seasonings, and natural sweeteners.

The accompanying web platform is engineered as a bespoke, ultra-responsive digital trade showcase built with modern semantic HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, crafted to reflect the corporate Movexa design standard (Navy `#07223e` / `#0A192F` and Ice Blue `#EDF5FD` with Warm Gold `#FDBE34` accents).

## 4. Infrastructure, Sourcing & Processing Facilities

### 4.1 Upstream Farmgate Procurement
* **12,000+ Contracted Acres:** Direct contractual partnerships with farmer producer organizations (FPOs) and cultivator cooperatives.
* **Direct Farm-Gate Aggregation:** Eliminates intermediaries to ensure competitive harvest-direct pricing and traceability down to the farm cluster level.
* **Pre-Harvest Residue Analysis:** Field agronomists test standing crops 14 days before harvest to ensure zero banned chemical residues.

### 4.2 Central Processing Terminal (Gujarat, India)
* **Buhler Optical Color Sorters:** German/Swiss engineered multi-camera optical grain and seed sorting equipment capable of detecting subtle discoloration, chalky grains, and foreign matter at a microscopic scale (<0.01% impurity tolerance).
* **Pneumatic Destoners & Gravity Separators:** Multi-tier vibrating sieves and air-density classifiers to isolate dust, chaff, immature seeds, and stones.
* **Continuous Steam Sterilization:** Validated heat and dry-steam treatment chambers reducing total bacterial plate counts (TPC), yeasts, molds, and coliforms without altering volatile oils or aromatic profiles.
* **Dehumidified Silos:** Moisture-controlled bulk storage maintaining seed equilibrium relative humidity (ERH) below critical fungal proliferation thresholds.

## 8. The 5-Stage Farm-to-Port Quality Protocol

The platform highlights the company’s signature closed-loop handling process designed to ensure 100% contract compliance:

```
[ Stage 01: Sourcing & Residue Testing ]
     ↓ Direct procurement from 12,000+ acres with pre-harvest HPLC residue screens
[ Stage 02: Sortex Cleaning & Dual NABL Lab Assay ]
     ↓ Buhler multi-camera optical sorters & magnetic destoning (<0.01% defects)
[ Stage 03: Export Packaging & Container Stuffing ]
     ↓ Nitrogen-flushed hermetic barrier bags & desiccant-lined ocean containers
[ Stage 04: Climate-Controlled Silo & Cold Storage ]
     ↓ Humidity-regulated storage protecting volatile oil integrity
[ Stage 05: Ocean Port Dispatch & Fast-Track Staging ]
     ↓ Direct 72-hr vessel staging at Mundra/Kandla with instant SGS clearance
```

1. **Stage 01 — Sourcing & Residue Testing:** Direct farm-gate procurement from contracted cultivator cooperatives across Gujarat and northern belts with pre-harvest residue checks.
2. **Stage 02 — Sortex & Quality Grading:** Multi-stage magnetic destoning, gravity separation, and lab titration for moisture, curcumin, aflatoxin, and pesticide traces.
3. **Stage 03 — Export Packaging & Dispatch:** Multi-wall kraft paper, airtight PP bags, and desiccated container stuffing at Mundra and Kandla ports with live GPS tracking.
4. **Stage 04 — Silo & Cold Storage:** Climate-controlled humidity-regulated silos preserving volatile oil content and preventing fungal development during staging.
5. **Stage 05 — Ocean Port Dispatch:** Direct port-gate clearance at Mundra & Kandla ports with complete phytosanitary, fumigation, and SGS inspection certificates.

## 14. Operational Workflows & Developer Reference

### 14.1 Running the Project Locally
The project requires no Node.js compilation or asset build steps:
1. Open the workspace root directory:
   ```powershell
   cd "c:\Users\techn\OneDrive\Desktop\vivek projects (1)\vivek projects\plantagroexports"
   ```
2. Serve via any local HTTP server (such as Python, VS Code Live Server, or `npx serve`):
   ```powershell
   python -m http.server 8000
   ```
   Or simply open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser.

### 14.2 Updating Product Catalog Items
To add or modify export commodities:
1. Open `index.html` and locate the `#productsSliderTrack` container (lines 475–748).
2. Duplicate or edit a `<article class="product-slide-card" data-category="...">` block.
3. Update the `data-category` attribute (`rice`, `spices`, `tea`, `pulses`, `dehydrated`) to ensure seamless filtering.
4. Add the corresponding `<option>` into `#inquiryProduct` (line 1145) to maintain automatic sync with the inquiry modal.

### 14.3 Adding Certifications or Trade Partners
* Certifications are managed in the `#about` section and the footer `.footer-cert-pills` element (`index.html`, lines 1082–1085).
* Testimonial cards are defined within `#testiSliderTrack` (`index.html`, lines 948–1045).
