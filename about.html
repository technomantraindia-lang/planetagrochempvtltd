About Planet Agro Chem

Planet Agro Chem is a trusted Indian exporter and supplier of high-quality agricultural commodities. Rooted in India’s rich agrarian landscape, we connect local farmers and certified producers directly with international buyers across the globe.

We handle end-to-end sourcing, stringent quality grading, packaging, and smooth export logistics. Our goal is simple: to deliver farm-fresh, pure, and export-standard Indian agro products on time, every time.

What We Export

We deal in bulk quantities of carefully selected agricultural products across 5 primary categories:

Grains & Cereals

Grains & Cereals

Products: Non-Basmati & Basmati Rice, Wheat, Corn.

Bulk shipment in 25/50kg BOPP & Jute bags with pre-shipment quality inspection.

Indian Spices

Spices

Products: Turmeric, Red Chilli, Cumin, Coriander, Black Pepper.

Machine-cleaned, Sortex graded, available whole or ground with certified low moisture.

Oilseeds & Pulses

Oilseeds & Pulses

Products: Sesame Seeds (White/Black), Groundnuts/Peanuts, Soybeans, Lentils, Chickpeas.

Naturally harvested, graded for purity, high oil content and export standards.

Fresh Produce

Fresh Produce & Processed

Products: Onions, Potatoes, Fresh Garlic.

Direct from Maharashtra farms, mesh bag ventilated packing for long sea-transit shelf life.

Fresh Fruits

Fresh Fruits

Products: Pomegranate, Mangos, Strawberries.

Carefully graded, corrugated export carton packing, cold-chain monitored for optimum freshness.
