THE 5-STAGE FARM-TO-PORT QUALITY PROTOCOL

Designed to guarantee 100% contract compliance, zero quarantine hold-ups, and immaculate laboratory assays upon destination port discharge.

01

Ethical Sourcing & Residue Test

Direct farm-gate procurement across 12,000+ contracted acres with pre-harvest HPLC residue screening.

02

Buhler Optical Sortex

High-resolution optical multi-camera color sorting and magnetic destoners achieving <0.01% defect tolerance.

03

Dual NABL Lab Testing

HPLC, GC-MS, and microbiological titration verifying zero heavy metals, low aflatoxins, and exact active assays.

04

Dehumidified Silo Staging

Equilibrium relative humidity (ERH) controlled storage preserving volatile aroma oils and preventing moisture absorption.

05

72-Hr Rapid Port Dispatch

Fast-track ocean vessel staging at Mundra & Kandla ports with Phytosanitary, Fumigation & SGS inspection.
